Tekcroft is committed to making our website accessible and usable for as many people as possible.

We aim to provide a website that can be accessed and used by people with different abilities, devices, and methods of navigating the web.

1. Our Approach

We consider accessibility as part of our ongoing website development and maintenance.

Our accessibility efforts may include areas such as:

  • Clear and consistent page structure
  • Readable text and appropriate colour contrast
  • Keyboard-accessible navigation
  • Alternative text for appropriate images
  • Clear labels and instructions for forms
  • Accessible links and interactive elements
  • Support for commonly used assistive technologies

We continue to review and improve the accessibility of our website as the site develops.

2. Accessibility Feedback

If you experience difficulty accessing any part of our website, please let us know.

Email:admin@tekcroft.com
Phone:+1 (800) 555-3100

When contacting us, please describe the page or feature you were trying to access and the difficulty you experienced. This will help us investigate the issue and identify an appropriate solution.

3. Alternative Access

If information on our website is not accessible to you, please contact us. Where reasonably possible, we will work with you to provide the information or assistance you need through an alternative method.

4. Updates

We may update this Accessibility Statement as our website, accessibility practices, or applicable requirements change.
