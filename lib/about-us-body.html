Why Tekcroft Exists

Software should make work easier.

Yet many businesses end up changing how they work to fit the software they bought. Our founders each saw that from a different side.

AD

Respected Mr Adnan

Co-founder | ran his own consultancy

Running his own consultancy, he had to deal with Excel sheets, repeated data entry and manual reporting. He tried automation to make the work easier. The trial became popular with colleagues and clients; as a result, the initial testing grew into products for property and healthcare.

AS

Respected Mr Adil

Co-founder | RE/MAX London

He met the problem from the inside. Rolling out property management and maintenance systems at RE/MAX London, he kept hitting tools that couldn't be customised and were tied to a vendor's way of working. Teams were juggling several systems when they needed one.

Tekcroft was the point where the two experiences met.

Where It BeganA1 Auto Spare Parts

Our first project. Orders ran through Excel sheets, separate warehouse software, phone calls and manual email follow-ups. After mapping their team's way of working, we built one platform on AWS, designed with our solution architect: call logging, B2B and B2C client management, website and vendor API integration, live checks, booking, payment and order completion.

That project set the pattern for everything since.

Live

From One Checklist to a Property EcosystemPropday

Propday began with a compliance checklist for a property client. Reporting dashboards followed, then sales and task management, each added due to the business needs. Today it covers property and lead management, finance, credit control and credit referencing, maintenance, inspections and compliance, keys and inventory, plus an email agent that reads each message, checks the database and replies in context. It supports RE/MAX franchises in the UK, and the same approach now runs solutions for medical and dental practices worldwide.

Synced

Growth, Built Into the System

Marketing follows the same logic. SEO, paid media, web and software don't run separately here. They feed one coordinated system, and reporting backs your business goals.

To power the SEO side, we are building KestrelClear, our own platform. It finds every SEO issue on a site, assigns each fix to a named team member and proves it worked, with the client able to see all of it.

How We Work

How we work 01020304 of four steps

Map

We map your process through workshops with leadership and teams, following the work from first call to completion to find the bottlenecks.

Decide

With our solution architect, we decide on the mix of software, automation, AI, web or mobile platform and marketing that fits your goals, so the investment matches your aim.

Build

Progress is reviewed across web, software, AI, SEO and paid media projects, removing blockers so the work stays on time and budget. We aim to deliver more than we promise.

Stay

Standard reporting and agreed KPIs show what's working and what isn't. We don't build and walk away.
